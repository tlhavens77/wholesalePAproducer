Place signature boxes

Your initials, signature, printed name and date, and both sellers’ boxes, are placed automatically on the initial and signature lines. Check them, drag any that are off, then send. To add one by hand, pick a box, then click on the page where it belongs. Initials boxes are placed on every page at once, in the same spot, so click the initial line on any page. Drag boxes to adjust; use × to remove one. Dates and printed names fill in automatically.
